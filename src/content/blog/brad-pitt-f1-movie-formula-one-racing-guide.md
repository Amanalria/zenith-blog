## Filming During Real Grand Prix Weekends: The Kosinski Method

Rather than constructing synthetic CGI race tracks or shooting on abandoned private airfields, Kosinski insisted on embedding the film crew directly inside the official 2023 and 2024 FIA Formula 1 World Championship seasons.

During the British Grand Prix at Silverstone, the Hungarian Grand Prix at the Hungaroring, and the night race on the Las Vegas Strip, APXGP operated as a genuine eleventh garage in the pit lane. Brad Pitt and Damson Idris suited up in custom fireproof race overalls, lining up behind Max Verstappen, Charles Leclerc, and Lewis Hamilton during official formation laps.

To capture the violent g-forces experienced by drivers at 220 miles per hour, Kosinski partnered with Sony to engineer the smallest, lightest full-frame 6K cinema cameras ever developed. Fifteen specialized optical lenses were mounted directly to modified Formula 2 chassis, transmitting real cockpit vibrations, steering wheel resistance, and tire degradation in blistering high resolution.

## Lewis Hamilton's Crucial Role as Co-Producer and Technical Guard

Hollywood racing movies historically alienate dedicated motorsport enthusiasts by inventing preposterous maneuvers—such as downshifting to accelerate on straightaways or surviving fatal crashes without a scratch. Lewis Hamilton signed on as producer through his production banner Dawn Apollo Films specifically to eradicate these Hollywood cliches.

Hamilton spent hundreds of hours in script revisions alongside screenwriter Ehren Kruger, ensuring dialogue reflects authentic tire management strategy, aerodynamic downforce physics, and telemetry communications. He coached Brad Pitt and Damson Idris through advanced simulator training at Mercedes headquarters in Brackley, teaching them to handle oversteer and braking points.

Hamilton insisted that the film reflect the hyper-competitive political landscape of modern Formula 1. The budget disputes, wind-tunnel restrictions, and cutthroat team orders mirror the cutthroat reality of modern motorsport, delivering high-stakes psychological tension off the track.

## The Car: Understanding the Custom APXGP Machinery

Audiences watching the movie are not looking at fiberglass props or mockups. The APXGP vehicle was designed and manufactured by Mercedes-AMG High Performance Powertrains and racing specialists Carlin Motorsport.

The team took a modern Dallara Formula 2 chassis and modified the aerodynamic sidepods, front wings, and suspension to match the elongated dimensions and ground-effect downforce of a modern Formula 1 car. The vehicles are powered by a turbocharged 3.4-liter Mecachrome V6 engine capable of producing over 620 brake horsepower.

Both Brad Pitt and Damson Idris attended a rigorous high-performance racing academy, accumulating over six hundred kilometers of solo track time before filming commenced. Pitt reached verified speeds exceeding 150 miles per hour through the high-speed Maggotts and Becketts corners at Silverstone, demonstrating authentic driving skill.

Apple struck a lucrative global distribution partnership with Warner Bros. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the official release date for Brad Pitt's F1 movie?
The movie *F1* will release in international movie theaters on June 25, 2025, and across North America on June 27, 2025, distributed by Warner Bros. Pictures and Apple Original Films.

### Did Brad Pitt really drive a real Formula 1 car during filming?
Brad Pitt drove a specially modified Formula 2 race car designed by Mercedes-AMG and Carlin Motorsport to look and handle like a Formula 1 car. Pitt drove solo on real F1 tracks at speeds exceeding 150 mph with 6K cinema cameras mounted to his chassis.

### Where was Brad Pitt's F1 movie filmed?
The production filmed on location during real race weekends at iconic Formula 1 circuits, including Silverstone (United Kingdom), Hungaroring (Hungary), Spa-Francorchamps (Belgium), Monza (Italy), Zandvoort (Netherlands), Las Vegas (USA), and Yas Marina (Abu Dhabi).

### What is the name of Brad Pitt's fictional F1 team in the movie?
Brad Pitt drives for the fictional eleventh Formula 1 team named **APXGP**, sporting a distinctive black, gold, and carbon-fiber livery sponsored by real-world motorsport brands.

### Where will the Brad Pitt F1 movie stream after theaters?
Following an exclusive global theatrical window in IMAX and movie theaters, *F1* will stream exclusively on **Apple TV+** worldwide in 4K HDR and Dolby Vision.

### What is Lewis Hamilton's role in the Brad Pitt F1 film?
Lewis Hamilton is a lead producer on the movie through his company Dawn Apollo Films. He personally audited the screenplay, ensured authentic technical and racing accuracy, and advised Brad Pitt on racing techniques and mental conditioning.

### Who is directing Brad Pitt's F1 film?
The film is directed by Joseph Kosinski, the visionary filmmaker behind the box office blockbuster *Top Gun: Maverick* (2022) and *Tron: Legacy* (2010).

### What is the budget of the Brad Pitt F1 movie?
Industry estimates place the production budget between $250 million and $300 million, largely due to high-tech camera development, production insurance, real track logistics, and strike-related filming interruptions.
