## 2. Spider-Man Surpasses The $2.4 Billion Milestone

Tom Holland’s *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* continued its juggernaut dominance, hauling in an astonishing $148.5 Million across 64 global territories over the weekend. The film officially crossed the coveted $2.4 Billion milestone ($2,410,200,000), making it the second highest-grossing superhero film of all time, behind only *Avengers: Endgame*.

Key drivers included IMAX and premium large format (PLF) re-attendance, fueled by viral social discussions surrounding the film's post-credit implications for *Avengers: Doomsday*. In North America alone, the film maintained a 62% screen occupancy rate across 4,200 venues.

## 3. Indian Box Office: Pan-India Action and Regional Mastery

The domestic Indian theatrical market experienced historic occupancies, generating over ₹390 Crore in cumulative gross collections over the three-day weekend:

### A. Rocking Star Yash Dominates with *Toxic*
Releasing on Friday, September 25 across four languages, *Toxic: A Fairytale for Grown-Ups* shattered single-day opening records in Karnataka and delivered phenomenal numbers across the Hindi heartland and overseas circuits. With a ₹78.20 Crore opening day and a ₹214.50 Crore three-day worldwide cume, the film secured the second-highest opening weekend for any Kannada-origin venture in cinema history.

### B. *The Vvaan* Wins Hindi Family Audiences
Balaji Motion Pictures' *The Vvaan: Force of the Forest* scored heavily across multiplexes and tier-2 single screens in North and Central India. Bolstered by spectacular visual effects and folklore mythology, Sidharth Malhotra and Tamannaah Bhatia registered a robust ₹43.80 Crore opening weekend against competitive counter-programming.

### C. *The Paradise* and *Mirzapur* Hold Relentless Steam
In Telugu states, Natural Star Nani’s period actioner *The Paradise* crossed ₹68.50 Crore worldwide within four days of release, proving the actor’s exceptional box office consistency. Meanwhile, *Mirzapur: The Movie* capitalized on its viral goodwill to cross ₹328.60 Crore worldwide by Sunday evening, proving that web series adaptations can dominate cinema halls when handled with cinematic scale.

## 4. Hollywood Mid-Budget Success: A24 & DreamWorks

Beyond superhero spectacles, mid-budget and animated titles enjoyed substantial counter-programming success:
* Universal/DreamWorks' *Forgotten Island* captured family crowds with an impressive $38.2 Million opening in North America.
* A24's Robert Pattinson-led psychological thriller *Primetime* scored a remarkable per-theater average of $22,400 across 850 specialty platform screens.
* Paramount’s wilderness thriller *Heart of the Beast* starring Brad Pitt secured $24.8 Million, driven by strong male demographic turnout aged 25–49.
